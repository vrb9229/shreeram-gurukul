Who we are

About Shreeram Gurukul

Shreeram Gurukul English Medium School is a pre-primary school located at Dongargaon, Taluka Sangola, District Solapur, Maharashtra. We welcome young children into a safe, caring and engaging environment in which every child feels comfortable, confident and genuinely happy to come to school.

Our approach is simple and child-centred: children learn through activities, exploration and meaningful experiences. Songs, stories, art, play and conversation are not extras — they are how young minds grow. Alongside this, our classrooms gently build language, early numeracy, social skills and the confidence to express oneself.

We believe that a child's earliest school years shape how they feel about learning for life. Our aim is to make those years warm, joyful and full of discovery.

  • A safe, caring and child-friendly environment
  • Learning through activities, exploration and experience
  • Focus on the all-round development of every child

Academics

Our Classes

Shreeram Gurukul English Medium School offers exactly three pre-primary classes — Nursery, Jr. KG and Sr. KG — each designed for the stage your child is growing through.

01

Nursery

A joyful first step into school and early learning.

A gentle introduction to routines, play and togetherness.
02

Jr. KG

Building confidence, curiosity and foundational skills.

Early language, numbers, expression and independence.
03

Sr. KG

Preparing children for the next stage of their educational journey.

Readiness, reasoning and school confidence for what comes next.

Our school offers three classes only: Nursery, Jr. KG and Sr. KG. Admission enquiries for all three classes are welcome.
